# Promise Form: ~ㄹ/을게(요)

The grammar form **~ㄹ/을게(요)** is a future tense variation used to make a **promise**, offer a favor, or kindly inform the listener of your decision. It often translates to "I'll do it for you," "Let me," or "I promise to."

## Formation
* **Vowel stem:** Add `ㄹ게요` (하다 -> 할게요)
* **Consonant stem:** Add `을게요` (놓다 -> 놓을게요)

## Usage and Rules

Because making a promise has specific logical constraints, this form comes with strict rules:

1. **First Person Only:** You can only make promises on your own behalf. The subject must be "I" or "We" (나/저 or 우리/저희).
2. **Action Verbs Only:** You can only promise to *do* something, not to *be* a certain way. Therefore, it is never used with descriptive verbs (adjectives). To say "I'll be pretty," you would say "I'll *become* pretty."
3. **Statements Only:** You cannot use this form to ask a question, because you don't ask someone else what *you* are promising.
4. **No Formal High Speech:** `~게(요)` acts as a context marker. Since `~습니다` is also a context marker, they cannot be combined. You cannot say `~을겝니다`. (To make a formal promise, use `~겠습니다` instead).

## Examples
* **제가 운동할게요.** (I will exercise. / I promise to exercise.)
* **제가 화장실 청소를 할게요.** (I'll clean the toilet for you. / Let me clean the toilet.)
* **저는 집에 혼자 갈게요.** (I'll just go home alone. -> Informing of decision.)
