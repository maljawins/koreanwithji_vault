# Likely / Surely: ~(으)리라(고)

The grammar form **~(으)리라(고)** is an older, sophisticated way to express assumptions, guesses, or firm determinations about the future. It is essentially the archaic equivalent of modern forms like `~겠다` or `~(으)ㄹ 것이다`. 

While not commonly used in everyday spoken Korean, it adds a "fancy," formal, or poetic tone and is frequently found in literature, songs, poetry, and stylized speech.

## 1. Construction
* **Consonant Stem:** + `~으리라고` (e.g., 먹다 -> 먹으리라고)
* **Vowel Stem:** + `~리라고` (e.g., 가다 -> 가리라고)
* **ㄹ-Irregular:** + `~리라고` (e.g., 살다 -> 살리라고)

## 2. Usage as a Quoting Form: ~(으)리라고
Because this form contains the quoting particle `~고`, it is almost always followed by verbs related to thinking, believing, saying, or determining (such as 생각하다, 믿다, 마음먹다, 결심하다, 말하다, 예상하다).
It can be thought of as a fancier replacement for `~겠다고` or `~(으)ㄹ 거라고`.

* **혼자서도 잘 하리라 믿어요.** (I believe that you will do well even by yourself.) 
  * *Equivalent to: 잘 하겠다고 믿어요.*
* **다시는 전화하지 않으리라 마음먹었어요.** (I made up my mind not to call again.)
* **다 먹을 수 있으리라고는 생각 못했어요.** (I never could have thought you would be able to eat all of that.)

## 3. Sentence-Final Usage: ~(으)리라
When the quoting particle `고` is omitted and the form is used at the end of a sentence, it functions as a poetic or formal way of stating that something "will" or "would" be the case. This usage is very rare in daily conversation but common in lyrics and poetry.

* **다섯 살 아이도 이 정도는 할 수 있으리라.** (Even a five-year-old child would be able to do this much.)
