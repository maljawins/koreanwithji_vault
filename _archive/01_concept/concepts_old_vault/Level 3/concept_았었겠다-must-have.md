# Must Have (Strong Assumption): ~았/었겠다

The structure **Past Stem + ~겠다** is used to make a strong assumption or guess about a past event, similar to "must have been" or "I bet that...". 

## Formation
Take the past tense stem (e.g., `했`, `갔`, `먹었`) and attach `~겠다`.
* 재미있다 -> 재미있었 -> **재미있었겠어요**
* 끝나다 -> 끝났 -> **끝났겠지**

## Usages

### 1. Strong Guess / "Must Have"
Compared to `~았/었을 거예요` (which sounds like a neutral prediction or "would have"), `~았/었겠다` carries a stronger sense of personal conviction or reaction. It often implies you are making a very certain guess based on evidence or empathy.
* 파티에 사람이 많이 왔어요. -> **재미있었겠어요.** (It must have been fun!)
* 한참 **찾았겠어요**. (It must have taken forever to find it.)
* 프랑스 코스 요리요? 정말 맛있**었겠네요**! (A French course meal? That must have been delicious!)

### 2. First-Person Hypothetical: ~았/었겠지(요)
When making a statement about what *you* (1st person) would have hypothetically done in the past, you cannot use `~겠어요`. Instead, it is a set rule to use the `~지(요)` ending -> **~았/었겠지(요)**. It means "I (probably) would have done it".
* 시간이 있었으면 (내가) **했겠지(요)**. (If I had time, I (probably) would have done it.)
* 진작 알았으면 같이 **갔겠지**. (If I knew beforehand, I (probably) would have gone.)
