**Memory Retention**

Now, if you try to memorize words with just the word and its definition, your brain is going to forget them in a matter of days, if not hours.

And there are two crucial steps you need to take to prevent this and to retain your memory.

1. You have to see the word used in an actual sentence.
2. You have to USE the word.

That's how you remember things in context.

**Step 3: Use an SRS Flashcard App**

Now, you can't just use any flashcard app. You need one with a Spaced Repetition System—SRS for short.

What it does is it tracks your brain's forgetting curve and shows you each word right when you're about to forget it.

And instead of just the word and its definition, you’re going to copy down your OWN sentence on the flashcard. Why? Once you see the sentence you wrote yourself right when you’re about to forget, it will remind you of how you used that word. And that context will make the meaning stick so much faster in your memory.
