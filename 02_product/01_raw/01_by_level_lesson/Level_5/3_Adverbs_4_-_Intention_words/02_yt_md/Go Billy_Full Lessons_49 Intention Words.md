## Teaching Points

### Overview

This lesson focuses on three essential Korean adverbs that describe the intention or lack thereof behind an action. While these are vocabulary items rather than grammar structures, the instructor emphasizes that they are equally important for natural communication. The adverbs covered allow speakers to express that an action was done in vain, intentionally, or by accident. Each word is presented with its English equivalent and practical example sentences to illustrate common usage in daily life.

---

### In Vain: 괜히

The adverb **괜히** is used to express that an action was done "in vain," was "pointless," or resulted in a "waste of time/effort." While the phrase "in vain" might feel slightly formal in casual English, **괜히** is a very common and natural expression in everyday Korean. It conveys a sense of regret or the realization that a previous action was unnecessary.

The instructor provides an example of buying something and later realizing it wasn't needed:
> **괜히 샀네요.** / You bought it in vain. (or "You wasted your money on that.")

Another scenario involves completing a task that turns out to be irrelevant, such as doing homework for a class that gets cancelled:
> **어제 괜히 숙제를 했어요.** / I did my homework in vain yesterday.

---

### On Purpose: 일부러

To describe an action done "on purpose" or "intentionally," Koreans use the adverb **일부러**. This word is used when someone makes a conscious choice to do (or not do) something.

A common conversational use is questioning someone's motives:
> **일부러 그랬지?** / You did that on purpose, right?

It can also describe a deliberate lack of action, as shown in the example of a sibling avoiding chores:
> **동생이 일부러 설거지를 안 했어요.** / My younger sibling didn't do the dishes on purpose.

---

### By Accident: 실수로

The opposite of doing something on purpose is doing it "by accident" or "accidentally," which is expressed using **실수로**. This word literally stems from the noun **실수 (mistake)** combined with the particle **로 (by means of)**.

The instructor demonstrates this with a situation where someone discards an item unintentionally:
> **제가 실수로 버렸어요.** / I threw it away on accident.

It is also frequently used for verbal slips or saying things one didn't mean to say:
> **실수로 그렇게 말했어요.** / I said that by accident.
