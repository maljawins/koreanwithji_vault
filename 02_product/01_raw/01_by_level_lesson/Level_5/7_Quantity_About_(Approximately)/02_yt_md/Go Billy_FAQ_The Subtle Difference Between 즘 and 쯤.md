## Teaching Points

### Overview
The instructor addresses a common point of confusion for Korean learners: the difference between the markers 쯤, 즈음, and 즘. While all three relate to the concept of "about," "around," or "approximately," they are used in different grammatical contexts and carry different levels of formality. The lesson is structured to first define the most common marker used with nouns, compare it to a similar synonym, and then explain the specific verb-based markers.

The learning arc moves from the simple attachment of 쯤 to nouns for general approximations of quantity and time, to the more specific use of 즈음 (and its shortened form 즘) for describing the approximate time an action occurs. By the end of the video, the viewer understands that 쯤 is for "about [amount]," while 즈음 is for "around the time [action] happens."

---

### The Approximation Marker 쯤
The marker 쯤 translates to "approximately," "around," or "about." It is used whenever a speaker is making a guess or an approximation regarding an amount, a number, or a specific time. Grammatically, it is attached directly to the end of a noun without any space. The instructor notes that 쯤 is considered an informal word; while it is perfectly acceptable for daily conversations with friends, colleagues, or even strangers, it should be avoided in formal speeches or academic essays.

Visual examples shown on screen demonstrate this direct attachment:
- **두 시간쯤** / about two hours
- **한 개쯤** / about one thing
- **육 개월쯤 공부했어요.** / I studied for about six months.

The instructor also highlights a specific idiomatic use with the verb 하다 (to do) to express approximate prices in casual conversation. For example, saying **천 원쯤 해요** means "It costs about a thousand won."

---

### 쯤 vs. 정도
The instructor briefly compares 쯤 with the word 정도, as they share similar meanings. However, there are key differences in usage and formality. While 쯤 is attached directly to a noun, 정도 is placed after a noun with a space. Furthermore, 정도 is appropriate for both formal and informal situations, making it more versatile than the casual 쯤. The instructor cautions that they are not always interchangeable; for instance, the grammar form 정도로 cannot be replaced by 쯤, and 정도 can be used with verbs, whereas 쯤 is strictly for nouns.

---

### Time-based Approximation: 즈음 and 즘
The words 즈음 and its shortened version 즘 also mean "about" or "around," but they are specifically used to indicate the approximate time that an event or action occurs. While 즈음 is occasionally seen after nouns, the instructor clarifies that this is less common and that 쯤 is the preferred choice for noun-based approximations.

The primary function of 즈음/즘 is to follow a verb to mean "around the time that [verb] happens." The construction requires taking an action verb stem and adding the future participle marker. If the stem ends in a consonant, you attach 을; if it ends in a vowel, you attach ㄹ. This is followed by a space and then either 즈음 or 즘.

The instructor provides a clear sentence example to illustrate this:
> **버스가 도착할 쯤 비가 왔어요.** / It rained around the time the bus arrived.

In this example, the marker follows the verb 도착하다 (to arrive), creating the meaning "around the time of arrival."
