## Teaching Points

**1. The Descriptive Verbs 있다 and 없다**
Ji explains that `있다` and `없다` are among the most important descriptive verbs in Korean. Their meaning changes based on the particle used:
*   **Possession:** When used with a subject marker (`이/가`), `있다` means "to have" (literally: "to be in existence to me").
    *   `나는 집이 있어요.` / I have a house.
*   **Existence/Location:** When used with the location marker `에`, it means "to be present at" or "to exist in."
    *   `나는 집에 있어요.` / I am at home.
*   **Action Verb Contrast:** Ji notes that while `가지다` also means "to have," it is an action verb and cannot be used in the present tense to express simple possession (e.g., `집을 가져요` is incorrect).

**2. Time and Location Particles: 에 vs. 에서**
Ji provides a detailed breakdown of these often-confused particles:
*   **에 (Time & Location):**
    *   **Time:** Marks a specific point in time (`오후 12시에` / at 12 PM). If multiple time nouns are used, `에` is only added to the final one.
    *   **Location (Movement):** Used when there is movement *to* a location (`가게에 가요` / go to the store).
    *   **Location (State):** Used with descriptive verbs to show where something exists (`책상에 열쇠가 있어요` / the key is on the desk).
    *   **Adverbial Exceptions:** Words like `어제 (yesterday)`, `오늘 (today)`, and `항상 (always)` are treated as adverbs and do not take `에`.
*   **에서 (Location Only):**
    *   **Occurrence:** Marks the location where an action takes place (`집에서 저녁을 먹었어요` / ate dinner at home).
    *   **Origin:** Means "from" or "out of" (`호주에서 왔어요` / came from Australia).
    *   **Abbreviation:** `에서` can be shortened to `서` after certain words like `어디서`, `여기서`, `저기서`, and `거기서`.

**3. Duration and Range: 부터 and 까지**
*   **까지 (Until/To):** Marks the end point of time or location (`저녁까지` / until evening; `회사까지` / to work).
*   **부터 (From/Since):** Primarily used for time to mark a starting point (`11시부터` / from 11 o'clock).
*   **Combinations:**
    *   `에서 ... 까지`: Used for location ranges (From A to B).
    *   `부터 ... 까지`: Used for both time and location ranges.
