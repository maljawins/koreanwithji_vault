## Teaching Points

### Overview
The instructor introduces the grammar form `~고 말다`, which is used to express the idea of "ending up doing" something. This lesson is specifically geared toward intermediate speakers, as it involves nuanced sentence structures that may be difficult for absolute beginners. The form is primarily used to describe an outcome that happened, often unexpectedly or despite a different original intention.

---

### How to Use ~고 말다
The construction of this form is straightforward: take a verb stem and attach the connector `고`, followed by the verb `말다`. In practice, this form is almost always conjugated into the past tense because it describes a completed action that resulted in a specific state. The instructor demonstrates this by showing the transformation on screen: **Verb Stem + 고 말다**.

A key pragmatic rule for using `~고 말다` is that it generally requires a preceding explanation. Just as in English, one would not typically say "I ended up falling asleep" without first explaining the context (e.g., "I was studying late"). The grammar form serves as the conclusion to a sequence of events, highlighting the final, often unintended, result.

---

### Examples and Context
The instructor provides several examples to illustrate how the form functions as a result of a prior situation. The first major example describes a student studying for a test who eventually loses the battle with exhaustion:
> **어제 밤에 시험 준비를 위해 공부하고 있었는데 잠들고 말았어요.** / Yesterday night I was studying to get ready for the test, but I ended up falling asleep.

In another example, the instructor uses the adverb `결국 (eventually/finally)` to emphasize the outcome of trying to eat a small amount but failing:
> **조금만 먹으려고 했는데 결국 다 먹고 말았어요.** / I was only trying to eat a little, but eventually I ended up eating all of it.

The form is also useful for describing mechanical failures or social mishaps. For instance, when a surprise is ruined or a machine breaks down:
> **깜짝 생일 파티를 준비하려고 했는데 다 들키고 말았어요.** / I was trying to prepare a surprise birthday party but it ended up being found out.
> **쿠폰을 프린트해 가려고 했는데 프린터기가 고장 나고 말았어요.** / I was trying to print out a coupon but the printer ended up breaking.

Finally, the instructor shows how it can be used for mistakes made during a task, such as cooking:
> **김밥을 만들려고 했는데 밥에 소금을 너무 많이 넣고 말았어요.** / I was trying to make kimbap, but I ended up adding too much salt.
