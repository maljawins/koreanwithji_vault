## Teaching Points
*   **Deciding and Promising (~기로 하다):**
    *   The particle **로** marks a choice. **~기로 하다** means "to set [action] as a choice," translated as "decided to" or "supposed to."
    *   Usually used in the past tense: *만나기로 했어요* (We decided to meet).
    *   Negative: *안 하기로 하다* or *하지 않기로 하다*.
*   **Starting and Stopping:**
    *   **~기 시작하다:** To start doing something.
    *   **그만 + Verb:** To stop doing something. **그만** is an adverb meaning "up to this point and no more." It is placed *before* the verb.
*   **Study Strategy (Input-Process-Output):**
    *   **Input:** Listen to a full podcast episode without stopping to grasp context.
    *   **Process:** Rewatch, pause, and analyze sentences using a dictionary (avoiding Papago for deep learning).
    *   **Output:** Shadow the speaker, reading out loud to match their speed.
