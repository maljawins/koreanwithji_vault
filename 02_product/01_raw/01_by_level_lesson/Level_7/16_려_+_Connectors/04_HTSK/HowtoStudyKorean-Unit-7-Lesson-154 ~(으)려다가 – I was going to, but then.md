Lesson 154: ~(으)려다가 – I was going to, but then

Introduction

In the [previous lesson](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit-7/lessons-151-158/lesson-153/), you saw that you can combine ~(으)려 with ~(으)니까 to combine their meanings. In this lesson, you will continue to expand your understanding of ~려 by connecting it to another grammatical principle.

~(으)려다가

In [Lesson 88](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/upper-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-4-lessons-84-91/lesson-88/), you saw that you can use ~다가 to indicate that one action halts and for another action to begin. For example:

Play[세면대를 청소하다가 갑자기 소변이 마려웠어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES001.mp3)
= I was cleaning the sink, and then all of a sudden I had to pee

You also saw in that lesson that, depending on the circumstance, the first action (which ends up halting) could be a cause of the second clause. For example:

Play[그 사람을 만나다가 가족의 소중함을 깨달았어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES002.mp3)
= After meeting that man (going out with that man), I realize the preciousness of family

Play[미남을 만나다가 외모의 중요성을 알게 됐어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES003.mp3)
= After meeting a handsome man (going out with a handsome man), I realized the importance of appearance/looks

The combination of ~(으)려 with ~다가 is a direct abbreviation of ~(으)려고 하다가, so the meaning of ~(으)려다가 is simply the combinations of their respective meanings. A very literal translation for this is “I intended to do a particular action, but then…” For example:

Play[대도시에 이사하려다가 비용이 너무 많이 들어서 포기했어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES004.mp3)
= I intended to move to the big city, but then gave up because it was too expensive

In practice, it is common to start these sentences in English with “I was going to…” Below are many more examples:

Play[한가지만 사려다가 여러 가지를 샀어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES005.mp3)
= I was going to buy just one thing, but I ended up buying several things

Play[상냥하게 말하려다가 화를 내고 말았어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES006.mp3)
= I was going to speak gently, but I ended up getting angry

Play[인상을 찌푸리려다가 상냥하게 웃었어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES007.mp3)
= I was about to furrow my brow, but I smiled gently instead

Play[먹구름이 끼려다가 갑자기 해가 나왔어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES008.mp3)
= Dark clouds were about to gather, but suddenly the sun came out

Play[초원에 가려다가 날씨가 안 좋아서 포기했어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES009.mp3)
= I was going to go to the meadow, but I gave up because of the bad weather

Play[사진을 선명하게 찍으려다가 카메라를 떨어뜨렸어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES010.mp3)
= I was going to take a clear picture, but I dropped the camera

Play[여중에서 강연을 하려다가 일정이 변경되어 여고로 갔어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES011.mp3)
= I was going to give a lecture at a girls’ middle school, but the schedule changed, so I went to a girls’ high school instead

Play[이 책의 저술자를 만나려다가 시간이 안 맞아서 못 만났어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES012.mp3)
= I was going to meet the author of that book, but the timing wasn’t right, so I couldn’t meet him

Play[동전을 자판기에 투입하려다가 기계가 고장 난 것을 알았어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES013.mp3)
= I was going to insert a coin into the vending machine when I realized it was broken

Play[세면도구를 챙기려다가 친구가 가져올 것 같아서 안 챙겼어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES014.mp3)
= I was going to bring my face-washing items but it was likely my friend was going to bring them so I didn’t

Play[매일 육식을 먹으려다가 건강을 위해서 채식으로 바꾸었어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES015.mp3)
= I was just going to eat meat every day, but for my health I changed it to vegetable (based)

Play[새 장치를 설치하려다가 기존 장치가 아직 쓸 만해서 그냥 두었어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES016.mp3)
= I was going to install a new device, but the existing one was still usable, so I left it as it was

Play[해적에 관한 이야기를 동생에게 하려다가 너무 무서울까 봐 그만뒀어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson154ES017.mp3)
= I was going to tell a pirate story, but I stopped because I thought it might be too scary
