Lesson 156: ~(으)려는 참 and ~(으)려던 참 – One was just about to

Introduction

In this lesson, you will continue to learn about grammatical principles that you can attach to ~(으)려, and how this can be used with the word 참. Let’s get started.

~(으)려는 참: To be about to do

In the previous lesson, you learned how you can add ~는 and 던 to ~(으)려 so you can combine their meanings. You saw how you can use the construction to describe an upcoming noun. A “noun” they often describe is 참. 참 isn’t a regular noun, but rather a pseudo-noun that can only be used when described by something. You have seen many pseudo-nouns in previous lessons:

적 in [Lesson 32](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit-2-lower-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-2-lessons-26-33/lesson-32/):
Play[우리 애기가 그렇게 소꿉장난한 적이 없어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES001.mp3)
= Our baby hasn’t played house like that before

수 in [Lesson 45](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit-2-lower-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-2-lessons-42-50/lesson-45/):
Play[그 돌이 너무 커서 던질 수 없어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES002.mp3)
= That rock is too big, so you can’t throw it

리 in [Lesson 133](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit-6/lessons-126-133/lesson-133/):
Play[그 사람이 너무 말라서 삼시 세끼를 먹을 리가 없어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES003.mp3)
= That person is so thin, there is no way she is eating three meals a day

참 can be used similarly to 적, 수 and 리 above. That is, it can only be used when described by something. 참 is usually described by ~(으)려는 or ~(으)려던. Just like the pseudo-nouns that you learned about in previous lessons, it’s hard to give 참 a meaning on its own. Rather, it makes more sense to give the entire construction of ~(으)려는 참 or ~(으)려던 참 a meaning.

You can use ~(으)려는 참 or ~(으)려던 참 to indicate that one was just about to do an action. If 참 is the last noun in the sentence and is predicated by 이다, the sentence itself can simply be used to indicate that one was just about to do an action. For example:

Play[캠핑에서 먹을 삼시 세끼를 준비하려는 참이에요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES004.mp3)
= I am about to prepare three meals a day for when we go camping

Play[은퇴식이 시작되는 참이에요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES005.mp3)
= The retirement party is just about to start

참 and the meaning it carries can also be put in the middle of a sentence. For example, ~에 is commonly added to 참 to indicate that some other event happened as one was about to do an action. The common translation for this usage is “when.” For example:

Play[창공을 바라보려는 참에 비행기가 지나갔어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES006.mp3)
= I was about to look at the blue sky when a plane passed by

Play[은퇴하려는 참에 새로운 프로젝트 제안을 받았어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES007.mp3)
= I was about to retire early when I received a new project proposal

Play[스티커를 떼려는 참에 종이가 찢어졌어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES008.mp3)
= I was about to remove the sticker when the paper tore

Play[돌을 치우려던 참에 비가 오기 시작했어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES009.mp3)
= I was about to remove the rock when it started to rain

Play[쓰레기통을 비우려던 참에 전화가 왔어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES010.mp3)
= I was about to empty the garbage can when the phone rang

Play[칼날을 갈려던 참에 전기가 나갔어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES011.mp3)
= I was about to sharpen the blade when the power went out

Play[강아지가 병들려는 참에 병원에 데려갔어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES012.mp3)
= The puppy was about to get sick when I took him to the hospital

Play[비가 갑자기 쏟아져서 옷이 흠뻑 젖으려던 참에 친구가 우산을 씌워 줬어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES013.mp3)
= I was about to get soaking wet when my friend covered me with an umbrella

Play[아이들이 소꿉장난을 하려던 참에 엄마가 저녁을 먹으라고 불렀어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES014.mp3)
= The children were about to play “house” when they were called for dinner

You can attach the grammatical principle of your choice to 참 to create a more complex meaning.

For example, you can add ~인데 (이다 + ㄴ데 from [Lesson 76](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/upper-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-4-lessons-76-83/lesson-76/) and [77](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/upper-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-4-lessons-76-83/lesson-77/)) to 참 to indicate that one is about to do something and this is information that the listener will need to understand next clause. For example:

Play[전세계를 여행하려는 참인데, 여행 경로에 대한 정보가 더 필요해요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES015.mp3)
= I am about to travel the whole world, but I need more information about travel routes

Play[여름날에 해변에 가려던 참인데 갑자기 비가 왔어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES016.mp3)
= I was about to go to the beach on a summer day, but it suddenly started raining

You can add 이니 (이다 + ~(으)니 from [Lesson 81](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/upper-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-4-lessons-76-83/lesson-81/)) to give background information. For example:

Play[중요한 예고를 하려는 참이니 모두 집중해 주세요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES017.mp3)
= I am about to make an important announcement, so please pay attention

You can add 인 듯 (이다 + ㄴ 듯 from [Lesson 67](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit-3-intermediate-korean-grammar/lessons-67-75/lesson-67/)) to indicate that it seems as though an action will happen. Inherently this is more commonly used when the acting agent isn’t the speaker, as it makes more sense to notice this of somebody else. For example:

Play[돌을 치우려는 참인 듯 힘을 주어 돌을 들어올렸어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES018.mp3)
= He seemed to be about to remove the rock, and he lifted it with effort

Play[중요한 예고를 하려던 참인 듯 그 남자는 잠시 망설였어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES019.mp3)
= That man seemed to be about to make an important announcement, and he hesitated for a moment

Play[쓰레기통을 비우려던 참인 듯 동생은 전화를 끊고 쓰레기 봉투를 들고 나왔어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Lesson156ES020.mp3)
= He seemed to be about to empty the garbage can, and then he hung up the phone and then brought out the garbage bags
