## Teaching Points

### 1. ~(으)니까 (Because/Since)
Ji introduces **~(으)니까** as a form that puts a much stronger emphasis on the reason itself compared to **~서**.
*   **Nuance:** It is used to explain a solid reason or personal opinion. Unlike **~서**, it does not require a natural flow or sequence between the two clauses.
*   **Grammar Rules:**
    *   **Suggestions/Commands:** Unlike **~서**, **~(으)니까** can be used with imperative and suggestive endings (e.g., **~자**, **~세요**).
    *   **Tense:** It can be attached to past tense stems (e.g., **했으니까**).
    *   **Emotions:** It cannot be used to express the reason for an emotion or greeting. For example, **만나니까 반갑습니다** is incorrect; one must use **만나서 반갑습니다**.
*   **Conjugation:**
    *   After a consonant (받침): **~으니까** (e.g., **먹다** → **먹으니까**).
    *   After a vowel: **~니까** (e.g., **가다** → **가니까**).
    *   Irregulars: **ㄹ**-drop (e.g., **살다** → **사니까**); **ㅂ** to **우** (e.g., **쉽다** → **쉬우니까**).
    *   **이다** (to be): **이니까** after a consonant, **니까** after a vowel (e.g., **친구니까**).

### 2. ~때문에 / ~기 때문에 (Because of / Because)
This is described as the most formal and direct way to state a reason, often found in news, writing, and formal speech.
*   **Noun + 때문에:** "Because of [Noun]."
*   **Verb Stem + 기 때문에:** "Because [Verb]." This uses nominalization (**~기**) to treat the action as a cause.
*   **Nuance:** It can sometimes carry a negative or accusatory connotation (blaming a cause).
*   **Positive Alternative:** Ji introduces **덕분에** (Thanks to) for positive reasons. Note: **덕분에** only works with nouns, not verbs (no **~기 덕분에**).
*   **Sentence Structure:** Ji demonstrates how to combine **~서** and **~때문에** in long sentences to avoid repetition, using **~서** for an intermediate reason and **~때문에** for the final cause.

### 3. Summary Table Comparison
Ji presents a comprehensive table comparing all four forms across several criteria:
*   **Past Stem:** Only **~(으)니까** and **~때문에** allow past tense stems.
*   **Commands/Suggestions:** **~(으)니까** is the primary choice; **~서** and **~때문에** are generally avoided.
*   **Emotions:** **~서** is the *only* form used for expressing the reason for emotions/greetings.
*   **Formality:** **~때문에** is the most formal; **~가지고** is strictly for spoken conversation.
