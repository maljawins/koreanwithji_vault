### Meaning and Usage of -다 보면
The grammar pattern **-다 보면** is used when a continuous or repeated action leads to a natural consequence or a new discovery. The instructor explains that if you keep doing the action in the first clause, the result in the second clause happens "on its own" or as a "new occurrence."

Visually, the instructor presents two primary rules on a digital chalkboard. First, the pattern indicates that continuing an action results in a state occurring naturally. For example, `자주 만나다 보면 호감이 생길 거예요 (If you meet often, you will develop feelings/liking for them)` and `매일 걷다 보면 건강해질 거예요 (If you walk every day, you will become healthy)`. 

Second, because the second clause describes a result that is expected to happen in the future based on current behavior, it is almost always paired with presumptive or speculative endings such as **-(으)ㄹ 수 있다 (can/might)** or **-(으)ㄹ 것이다 (will/is likely to)**. This is demonstrated with the example `소리 내어 읽다 보면 발음이 좋아질 거예요 (If you keep reading aloud, your pronunciation will improve)`.

### Conjugation and Practice
The pattern **-다 보면** is used exclusively with verbs. The conjugation is straightforward because it does not change based on whether the verb stem ends in a consonant (받침) or a vowel. The instructor provides a list of verbs for the student to practice transforming:

- **오다 (to come)** → **오다 보면**
- **읽다 (to read)** → **읽다 보면**
- **걷다 (to walk)** → **걷다 보면**
- **배우다 (to learn)** → **배우다 보면**
- **만들다 (to make)** → **만들다 보면**
- **공공부하다 (to study)** → **공부하다 보면**
