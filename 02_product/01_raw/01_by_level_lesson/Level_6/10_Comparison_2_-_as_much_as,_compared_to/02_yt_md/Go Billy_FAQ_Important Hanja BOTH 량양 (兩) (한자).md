### The Character: 兩 (량/양)

The character **兩** is read as **량** or **양**. Due to the Korean sound change rule known as 두음 법칙 (Initial Sound Rule), the character is pronounced and written as **양** when it appears at the beginning of a word. Visually, the character is thought to be a drawing of two horses yoked together, or simply the same object drawn twice to represent a pair. 

Historically, this character took on additional meanings related to measurement. Because it resembles a scale, it became a unit of weight. Specifically, one **량** was equivalent to 37.3 grams. Furthermore, **량** served as the name for Korean currency between 1892 and 1902, used before the introduction of the current **원 (won)**.

The instructor provides a cautionary note regarding the word **양말 (socks)**. Although socks come in pairs, the **양** in **양말** comes from a different Hanja character entirely, so learners should not assume every word starting with **양** relates to this specific "both" character.

---

### Common Vocabulary Using 兩

The instructor lists and explains several high-frequency words where **양** signifies "both" or "two."

- **양쪽 (both sides):** Combines **양** (both) with **쪽** (side/direction). It can refer to physical sides, two different parties in a negotiation, or two different ways.
- **양발 (both feet):** Combines **양** (both) with **발** (foot).
- **양팔 (both arms):** Combines **양** (both) with **팔** (arm).
- **양친 (both parents):** Uses the character **친** (familiar/family). While it literally refers to "both family members," it is the formal term for both parents.
- **양가 (both families/households):** Uses the character **가** (house/household) to refer to two different families, often used in the context of marriage or formal meetings between households.
- **양반 (nobility/aristocracy):** A historical term for the ruling class. Originally, there were two branches (**반**) of nobility in Korea: the civil branch and the military branch. **양반** referred to "both branches" before evolving into a general term for the noble class.
