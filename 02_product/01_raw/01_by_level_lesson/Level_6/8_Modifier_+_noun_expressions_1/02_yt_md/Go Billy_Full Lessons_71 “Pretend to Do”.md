## Teaching Points

### Overview
The instructor introduces the verb 척하다, which is used to express that someone is pretending to do something or acting as if a certain state is true. This grammar point is highly versatile as it can be combined with both adjectives and action verbs to describe a wide range of deceptive or performative behaviors.

The lesson follows a structured progression: first defining the core verb, then explaining the conjugation rules for adjectives, and finally detailing how to use the form with action verbs. Throughout the video, the instructor provides practical example sentences that illustrate common social situations, such as pretending to be asleep or acting like one doesn't know someone.

---

### Using 척하다 with Adjectives
To say someone "pretends to be [adjective]," you must first change the adjective into its noun-modifying form and then add 척하다. The instructor demonstrates this with the English example "I pretended that I was cold." In Korean, the adjective 춥다 (to be cold) becomes 추운, resulting in the phrase 추운 척하다. The final verb 척하다 can then be conjugated into any tense or politeness level needed for the sentence.

The instructor provides several examples of this adjective construction:
- **추운 척했어요.** / I pretended that I was cold. (or "I acted like I was cold.")
- **안 무서운 척했어요.** / I pretended that I wasn't afraid.
- **왜 아픈 척을 했어요?** / Why did you pretend you were hurt?
- **귀여운 척하지 마.** / Don't act cute.

Note that the object marker 을 can optionally be attached to 척 (as in 척을 하다), though it is frequently omitted in casual speech.

---

### Using 척하다 with Action Verbs
When expressing that someone "pretends to [do an action]," the construction changes slightly. You take the action verb stem and attach 는 척하다. For example, the verb 먹다 (to eat) becomes 먹는 척하다, and 하다 (to do) becomes 하는 척하다.

There is a specific rule for verbs ending in a ㄹ batchim: you must remove the ㄹ before adding 는. The instructor uses the verb 알다 (to know) to illustrate this: the ㄹ is dropped, resulting in 아는 척하다.

The instructor shares several action-based examples:
- **밖에서 저를 아는 척하지 말아 주세요.** / Please don't act like you know me outside (of here).
- **대화를 하기 싫어서 자는 척했어요.** / I didn't want to talk, so I pretended to be asleep.
- **저 사람은 고빌리의 동영상을 보면서 공부하는 척을 해요.** / That person pretends to study while watching Go Billy's videos.
