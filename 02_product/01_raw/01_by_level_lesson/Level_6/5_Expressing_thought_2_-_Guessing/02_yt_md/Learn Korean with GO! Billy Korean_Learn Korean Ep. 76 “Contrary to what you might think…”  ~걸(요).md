### Construction and Pronunciation
To create this form, you start with a verb stem. If the verb stem ends in a vowel, you attach the consonant ㄹ followed by 걸. If the verb stem ends in a consonant, you attach 을 followed by 걸. To make the expression more polite, you can add the suffix 요 to the end, resulting in ~걸요.

A critical pronunciation note is provided: although the form is written as 걸, it is actually pronounced as 걸 (with a tensed 'k' sound) because it follows the ㄹ sound. This phonological shift is a standard feature of this specific grammar structure.

### Meaning and Usage: Disagreeing with Expectations
While there is no single literal translation in English, the best way to conceptualize ~걸(요) is as meaning "contrary to what you might expect" or "contrary to what you might think." It is primarily used to disagree with the listener's current thoughts or expectations. When speaking, it is common to use a rising intonation at the end of the sentence to signal this corrective nuance.

The instructor provides four distinct examples to illustrate this corrective function:
- **아닐 걸요?** / Contrary to what you might think, it's not. (Used to correct someone who thinks something is true).
- **이미 도착했을 걸요?** / He would have already arrived. (Used to correct someone who thinks the person hasn't arrived yet).
- **지금 가면 늦을 걸요?** / If you go now, you'll be late. (Used to correct someone who thinks they will make it on time).
- **그그렇게 하면 별로 맛 없을 걸요?** / It won't taste so good if you do that. (Used to correct someone who thinks their method will result in good flavor).

---

### Social Context and Politeness
The instructor cautions that this form is generally not appropriate for strictly formal situations. Even with the addition of the polite ending 요, the inherent nature of the grammar—telling someone that they are wrong or that their thinking is mistaken—can come across as blunt or rude in a formal hierarchy. However, it is perfectly acceptable and very common in general daily situations and when speaking with friends or acquaintances.
