**Specific usage of Past Tense in Korean.**

Let’s look at this sentence.

“제 남자친구는 잘생겼어요” – My boyfriend is handsome.

As you can tell, it is in the past tense. But why though? Shouldn’t it be just 잘생겨요?

Now, 잘생기다 is a combination of 잘 meaning “well” and 생기다, which means “To take form.”

And the reason why 잘생기다 is used always in the past is because current state of being handsome has to have come from the result of a past process (like having been born that way or developing that appearance).

In other words, the process of “being formed well” must have happened in some time in the past.

So technically speaking, 잘생겨요 in the present tense would mean he is “well-forming” which doesn’t make sense.

This is why you need to say your boyfriend is "well-formed - 잘생겼어요". And that state continues, so as a result, he IS handsome.

Then how do you ACTUALLY say “He WAS handsome”?

It’s very simple.

We talked about the past stem in level 1.

In our case, “잘생겼” is the past stem.

All you need to do is add another “었” after “잘생겼” so now we get “잘생겼었어요.”

This is called double past tense which we’re going to talk about in just a minute because I want to share with you another example that works exactly like this.

“미쳤어?” means “Are you crazy?” which comes from 미치다 - an action verb which means “To GO crazy”.

And in order for someone to BE crazy in the present moment, they must have “had GONE” crazy at some point in the past. And as a result, they ARE crazy right now.

This might sound a bit counter-intuitive to you because English generally separates the initiation (past) from the current state (present).

However, in Korean, there are certain verbs that are put into the past tense form like this to describe the current state that results from a completed action from the past.

Which is why I shared with you a list of verbs that work exactly like this that you can download.

**Double Past Tense**

설명 보충 필요.

엄마는 미국에 갔다. This does not rule out the possibility that Mom is still currently in America.

엄마는 미국에 갔었다. This means Mom have visited America in the past, but since then she has returned.
