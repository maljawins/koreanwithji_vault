## Sheet1
| Korean Past Tense Form | English Translation (Contextual Present Tense) | Basic Form (Dictionary Form) | Explanation/Logic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 잘생겼어요 (Jal-saeng-gyeoss-eo-yo) | "is handsome" / "is good-looking" | 잘생기다 (Jal-saeng-gi-da) | This form describes the current state of being handsome, which is understood as the continuing result of a process initiated in the past (e.g., being born that way). |
| 닮았어요 (Dal-mass-eo-yo) | "looks like" / "resembles" | 닮다 (Dal-mda) | The resemblance (the similarity) is perceived as the result of a past event (like meeting or inheriting traits), and it continues to hold true in the present. |
| 말랐어요 (Mal-lass-eo-yo) | "is skinny" | 마르다 (Ma-reu-da) | This form is used when talking about someone being skinny, reflecting their current condition. If used in the present tense (말라요), it might refer to a physical attribute that is drying up, like hair. |
| 틀렸어요 (Teul-lyeoss-eo-yo) | "is wrong" | 틀리다 (Teul-li-da) | This indicates that something is incorrect in the present. The present tense form, 틀려요, can be acceptable but 틀렸어요 is often preferred to mean "it is wrong". |
| 맞았어요 (Maj-ass-eo-yo) | "is right" / "is correct" | 맞다 (Mat-da) | Similar to 틀렸어요, this uses the past tense conjugation to express the current state of being correct. |
