So, Future tense in Korean has several endings with each meaning slightly different meanings.

ㄹ것이다 when used as a future tense, is the most standard future tense with not much contextual remark.

But it can mean something different depending on the context, but let’s first look at general rules to use this ending

1. ㄹ 받침 signifies something incomplete like [something to be done], meaning it already indicates future tense, you never conjugate 이다 to future tense separately.
2. Stem이 받침이 있다면 을이 붙고 받침이 없으면 ㄹ받침이 들어간다.
3. 그리고 것이다는 거다로 shorten 많이 시킨다. ‘것이다’는 일상 대화에서 많이 쓰이지 않는다.
4. 또한 ‘거’는 ‘꺼’로 발음된다 just like we learned mine yours 내꺼 네꺼.
5. 그리고 that 것이다 literally means "Thing" and "to be".

So, 가다, because the stem 가 doesn’t have 받침, becomes 갈 거야 in 반말, informal low, 갈 거예요 in informal high, 갈 겁니다 in formal high. Repeat after me. 나는 파티에 갈 거야 / 저는 파티에 갈 거예요 / 저는 파티에 갈 겁니다.

In honorific, 시 comes in right after the stem, so it becomes 가실 거야 가실 거예요 and 가실 겁니다

앉다 has 받침 so ‘을’ has to be added, so it becomes 앉을 거야 앉을 거예요 앉을 겁니다. And in honorific, 으시 comes in after the stem. So, it becomes 앉으실 거야 앉으실 거예요 앉으실 겁니다.

So, depending on the context and the particle, it could mean "It is something to do"

For example, 사과를 먹을 거예요 means I will eat the apple but 이것은 먹을 거예요 means this is something to eat.

So, particle and context is important.
