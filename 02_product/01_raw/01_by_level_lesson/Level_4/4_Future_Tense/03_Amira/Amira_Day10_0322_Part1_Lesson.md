## Teaching Points

- **The Complexity of the Future Tense:** Ji explains that the future is inherently uncertain, which leads to various endings based on the speaker's perspective. He compares this to English nuances:
    - "I'm coming" (casual/immediate).
    - "I'm going to come" (future timeline emphasis).
    - "I will come" (strong determination/promise).
    - "I should come" (prediction/probability).

- **The Standard Future: ㄹ 것이다 / 거다:**
    - **Structure:** Stem + ㄹ/을 + 것이다 (shortened to 거다 in conversation).
    - **Mechanics:** The ㄹ/을 suffix itself indicates the future/incomplete state. Because the future is already marked by the suffix, the following verb 이다 (to be) is conjugated in the present tense (예요/이야/입니다).
    - **Pronunciation:** The 거 in this construction is pronounced with a tensed sound as 꺼.
    - **Meanings:**
        1. **Simple Future:** "I will..." e.g., 사과를 먹을 거예요 (I will eat an apple).
        2. **Modifier (Thing to do):** "Something to..." e.g., 이것은 먹을 거예요 (This is something to eat).
        3. **Prediction/Guess:** "Should/Must be..." e.g., 지혜가 알 거야 (Jihye should know/must know).

- **Conjugation Rules for ㄹ 거다:**
    - If the stem ends in a vowel: Add ㄹ (e.g., 가다 → 갈 거예요).
    - If the stem ends in a consonant (받침): Add 을 (e.g., 먹다 → 먹을 거예요).
