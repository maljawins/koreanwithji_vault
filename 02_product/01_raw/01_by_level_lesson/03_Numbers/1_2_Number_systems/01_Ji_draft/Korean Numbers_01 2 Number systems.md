**Introduction**

In this section of the course, I'm going to be covering almost everything you need to know about Korean numbers in depth including how to count, tell dates and time and age.

Numbers in Korean can be confusing for an English speaker to learn because there are two number systems in Korean – Native or Pure Korean number and Sino-Korean number.

"Sino-Korean" means it's derived from Chinese characters.

This goes back to when Korea used the Chinese writing system before King Sejong invented 한글, the Korean alphabet.

**Three Types of Numbers**

Before we get into it, there's a concept we need to cover first: Cardinal, ordinal, and nominal numbers.

These are actually the three different types of numbers that exist universally.

**Cardinal Numbers**

Cardinal numbers are for counting. For example, here we have one, two, three apples.

[Show: 3 apples]

So in this case, "three" is a cardinal number.

**Ordinal Numbers**

Ordinal numbers, as the name suggests, are for ordering—like first, second, and third apple.

**Nominal Numbers**

And finally, we have nominal numbers.

These are things like phone numbers, zip codes, passwords—numbers that are just for naming stuff, not related to actual quantity.

**How English Uses These**

In English, we have ONE number system—"one, two, three"—for both cardinal and nominal numbers.

And we use "first, second, third" and so on for ordinal numbers.

**How Korean Uses These**

In Korean though, we use Native Korean numbers for cardinal numbers and Sino-Korean numbers for nominal numbers.

And ordinal numbers are very similar to our cardinal numbers.

**Two Words for "Number"**

Similarly, we have two different words that mean "number" in Korean: 번호 and 숫자.

And they're used for different purposes.

번호 refers to numbers used to identify or label items in a sequence.

숫자 refers to the numerical symbols themselves—like 0, 1, 2—representing quantity.

Simply put: 번호 has a purpose. 숫자 is just the name of the numbers.

For example, "phone number" in Korean is 전화번호, not 전화숫자.

010-1234-1234 is the entire 전화번호 (phone number).

010 is a 숫자 (numeral)—a part of that 전화번호
