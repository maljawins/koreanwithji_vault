Lesson 161: Describing Upcoming Nouns with ~느냐는

Introduction

In this lesson, you will learn how to describe upcoming nouns with ~느냐, which you learned in the previous lesson. Let’s get started.

~느냐는: Describing Upcoming Nouns

In [Lesson 26](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit-2-lower-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-2-lessons-26-33/lesson-26/) and [27](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit-2-lower-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-2-lessons-26-33/lesson-27/), you saw how you can use ~는 to describe an upcoming noun. For example:

Play[진주가 있는 굴을 찾고 있어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s1.mp3)
= I’m looking for an oyster with a pearl in it

In [Lesson 155](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit-7/lessons-151-158/lesson-155/), you saw how you can do this by attaching ~는 to more complex grammatical principles like ~(으)려. For example:

Play[층간 소음 때문에 쿵쿵 소리를 안 내려는 사람이 많아요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s2.mp3)
= There are a lot of people who are trying not to hear banging because of neighbors above them in apartments

In this same way, you can encapsulate the meaning of ~느냐 and its preceding clause to describe an upcoming noun. For example:

Play[계획이 실행되느냐 안 되느냐는 결정을 기다려야 돼요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s3.mp3)
= We need to wait on the decision of if the plans would be carried out or not

Here, the word 결정 is being described by the entire part of the sentence before it. This includes the speculative connotation of ~느냐. Below are many examples of this in use:

Play[그의 거부가 정당하냐는 논의가 이어졌어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s4.mp3)
= The debate over whether his refusal was justified continued

Play[이 규칙이 공정하느냐는 판단을 내리기 어려워요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s5.mp3)
= It’s hard to judge whether this rule is fair

Play[그가 회사에 복귀하느냐는 논의가 계속되고 있어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s6.mp3)
= The discussion of whether he will return to the company is ongoing

Play[쇠가 부식되느냐는 문제는 산업에서 매우 중요해요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s7.mp3)
= The issue of whether iron corrodes is very important in the industry

Play[이 관습이 언제부터 시작되었느냐는 논쟁이 많아요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s8.mp3)
= There is a lot of debate about when this custom started

Play[그가 진심으로 사과하느냐는 논란의 중심에 있었어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s9.mp3)
= The controversy centered on whether he sincerely apologized

Play[이 프로젝트가 성공하느냐는 회의의 주요 주제였어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s10.mp3)
= The main topic of the meeting was whether this project would succeed

Play[우리가 이 문제를 해결 할 수 있느냐는 논의가 필요해요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s11.mp3)
= Discussion is needed about whether we can solve this problem

Play[그녀가 정말 청순하느냐는 소문 때문에 온 동네가 떠들썩했어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s12.mp3)
= The rumor about whether she is really innocent/pure made the whole neighborhood noisy

Play[괴물이 정말 존재하느냐는 의문이 아직도 풀리지 않았어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s13.mp3)
= The question of whether the monster truly exists has not yet been answered

Play[악마가 정말 존재하느냐는 이야기가 사람들 사이에서 퍼졌어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s14.mp3)
= The story of whether the devil truly exists spread among people

Play[국수가 제대로 익었느냐는 평가가 요리 대회에서 중요한 기준이었어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s15.mp3)
= The evaluation of whether the noodles were properly cooked was an important standard in the cooking contest

Play[우리가 이번 프로젝트를 제시간에 끝낼 수 있느냐는 질문이 제기되었어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Lesson161s16.mp3)
= The question of whether we can finish this project has been raised
