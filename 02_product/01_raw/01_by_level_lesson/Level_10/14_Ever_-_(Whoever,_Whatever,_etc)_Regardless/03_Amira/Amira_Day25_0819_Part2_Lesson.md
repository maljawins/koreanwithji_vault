## Teaching Points

*   **~(으)며 (And/While):** A formal connector used primarily in writing, formal speeches, or poetic contexts like song lyrics.
    *   **With Descriptive Verbs:** It means "and" (listing). Example: 이 컴퓨터는 빠르며 용량이 커요 (This computer is fast and has a large storage). In speech, ~고 is preferred.
    *   **With Action Verbs:** It means "while" (simultaneous action). Example: 샤워를 하며 TV를 봤어요 (I watched TV while taking a shower). In speech, ~면서 is the standard choice.
    *   **Special Spoken Usage:** The quoting form + 며 (often pronounced as "매" in the Gyeonggi dialect) is used to confirm or contradict information. Example: 스타벅스 싫다며? (I thought you said you didn't like Starbucks?).
*   **Indirect Questions (Modifier + 지):** Uses the bound noun 지 to embed a question within a larger sentence.
    *   **Conjugation:** Present Action (~는지), Present Descriptive (~ㄴ/은지), Past (~았/었는지), and Future (~ㄹ/을지).
    *   **Usage:** Commonly paired with verbs of information like 알다 (to know), 모르다 (to not know), or 궁금하다 (to be curious). Example: 화장실이 어디 있는지 아시나요? (Do you know where the bathroom is?).
*   **어쩐지 vs. 왠지:** Both translate to "somehow" or "for some reason," but have different nuances.
    *   **어쩐지:** Retrospective and certain. Used when a prior feeling is confirmed. "No wonder..."
    *   **왠지:** Speculative. Used for a present feeling about something that might happen. "For some reason, I feel like..."
*   **~든지 (Regardless/Choice):** Indicates a choice between options where any choice is acceptable.
    *   **With Question Words:** Creates "~ever" meanings: 누구든지 (whoever), 어디든지 (wherever), 언제든지 (whenever).
    *   **Vs. ~던지:** Ji warns against confusing 든지 (choice) with 던지 (past recollection of a feeling/degree).
    *   **~든가:** Essentially interchangeable with 든지, though 든지 sounds more natural after a noun. Ending a sentence with 든지/든가 conveys an indifferent "do it or don't" attitude.
*   **ㄴ/은 지 되다 (Time Since):** Indicates the duration of time since a completed action. Ji emphasizes the **required space** before 지 to distinguish it from the indirect question marker. Example: 한국에 온 지 3년이 넘었어요 (It has been over 3 years since I came to Korea).

## Lesson Flow
