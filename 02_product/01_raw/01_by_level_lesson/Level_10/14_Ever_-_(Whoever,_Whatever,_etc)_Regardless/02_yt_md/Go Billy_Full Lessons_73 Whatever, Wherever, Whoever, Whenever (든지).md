## Teaching Points

### Overview
The instructor introduces a simple and consistent method for creating "ever" words in Korean, such as whatever, wherever, whoever, and whenever. Rather than memorizing these as four unrelated vocabulary items, the lesson focuses on a single grammatical attachment that can be added to standard interrogative words (who, what, where, when).

The core of the lesson is the suffix 던지. By attaching this suffix to a question word, the speaker transforms a specific inquiry into an indefinite, all-encompassing expression. The video provides a clear formula for construction and demonstrates practical usage through example sentences that highlight common conversational nuances.

---

### Constructing "Ever" Words with 던지
The instructor explains that making these words is simple if you remember the suffix 던지. To create the desired expression, you take the basic Korean question word and attach 던지 directly to the end of it.

- **뭐 (what) + 던지 = 뭐든지 (whatever)**
- **어디 (where) + 던지 = 어디든지 (wherever)**
- **누구 (who) + 던지 = 누구든지 (whoever/anyone)**
- **언제 (when) + 던지 = 언제든지 (whenever)**

This pattern allows learners to quickly expand their vocabulary by applying one rule to words they likely already know. The instructor emphasizes that this is a much more efficient way to learn these terms than treating them as isolated words.

---

### Practical Examples and Nuances
The lesson concludes with four example sentences to show how these words function in natural speech.

> **저는 뭐든지 다 잘 먹어요.** / I can eat whatever.

The instructor notes a specific cultural nuance here: while **잘 먹다** literally translates to "to eat well," in this context it means the speaker enjoys eating everything or has no problems eating any kind of food (i.e., they are not a picky eater).

> **어디든지 여행 가고 싶어요.** / I want to travel wherever.

This demonstrates the use of **어디든지** to express a lack of preference for a specific destination, emphasizing the desire for travel itself.

> **누구든지 할 수 있어요.** / Anyone can do it.

While **누구든지** literally means "whoever," the instructor points out that "anyone" is often a more natural English translation in sentences expressing capability or general possibility.

> **언제든지 전화하세요.** / Call me whenever.

This final example shows **언제든지** used to offer flexibility and openness regarding time.
