## Teaching Points

### Overview

The instructor introduces the word 바탕, which is a regular noun meaning "foundation," "basis," "background," or "ground." While it is a standard noun, it is frequently used in a specific grammatical construction to express that one thing is based on another. This lesson focuses on the structure Noun + 을/를 바탕으로 and its various applications in both formal and informal Korean.

The learning arc begins with the basic mechanics of the form, moves into how to use verbs as the basis of an action, explores the "hidden" verb 하다 that appears in adjectival forms, and concludes with several synonyms that carry slightly different nuances. Throughout the video, the instructor emphasizes that while this form is common, it carries a slightly "fancy" or formal tone compared to simpler alternatives.

---

### Basic Structure and Usage

To say something is "based on" a noun, you take the noun, attach the object marker 을 or 를, and follow it with 바탕으로. The instructor illustrates this on the whiteboard with the formula: **Noun(을/를) 바탕으로**. For example, if something is based on a book (책), the phrase becomes 책을 바탕으로.

A key rule for this construction is that it must be followed by an action verb. The sentence describes doing an action based on a specific foundation. Technically, the instructor notes that the verb 하다 (in its formal 하여 or informal 해 form) is "hiding" inside this expression, though it is omitted 99% of the time in speech and writing.

---

### Using Verbs with 바탕으로

If the basis for an action is not a simple noun but a verb (e.g., "based on what I heard"), you must first transform that verb into a noun. This is done using the ~는 것 (the thing/fact of) structure. The instructor demonstrates this by writing **것을** (often shortened to **걸**) on the board.

For example, to say "based on what I heard," you take the verb 듣다 (to hear), change it to the past tense modifier 들은, add 것, and then apply the 바탕으로 structure: 들은 것을 바탕으로 or 들은 걸 바탕으로. This allows for complex foundations like feedback received from friends or information gathered from witnesses.

---

### Formal Nuance and Everyday Alternatives

The instructor explains that 바탕으로 is primarily used in "fancy" or formal speech. While it is perfectly fine in polite conversation, it can feel a bit heavy for everyday situations. If you want a more casual, "everyday" feeling, you can use two simpler alternatives:

1. **으로/로**: This particle means "using" or "with." Instead of saying "I studied based on past exams," you can simply say "I studied using past exams" (기출문제로 공부했어요).
2. **가지고**: This is used after a noun with the object marker (Noun + 을/를 가지고) to mean "using" or "with."

Using these alternatives conveys a similar meaning but loses the specific nuance of something being the "background" or "basis" of the action.

---

### Describing Nouns: The Adjectival Form

When you want to use "based on" to describe a noun (e.g., "a movie based on a true story"), the "hidden" verb 하다 must reappear. In this case, you use the form **~를 바탕으로 한**.

The instructor provides a visual example on the board:
> **이건 실화를 바탕으로 한 영화예요.** / This is a movie based on a true story.

Here, 실화 (true story) is the foundation, and 한 (the adjective form of 하다) links the basis to the noun 영화 (movie). Without the 한, the sentence would be grammatically incomplete when functioning as a modifier.

---

### Synonyms and Nuance Differences

The instructor introduces three synonyms for 바탕 that are used in the exact same grammatical way (Noun + 을/를 [Synonym]으로). While they all mean "based on," they carry distinct physical or conceptual nuances:

- **토대로**: Derived from 토대 (foundation/base of a building). It carries the nuance of a "structure" or a solid physical base.
- **기초로**: Derived from 기초 (basics). It carries the nuance of a "starting point." Using this suggests that the noun was the initial foundation, but further work was built upon it.
- **기반으로**: Derived from 기반 (base/ground, like a military base). It refers to an actual physical or organizational base.

The instructor demonstrates these with examples like 설문조사 결과를 기초로 (based on survey results as a starting point) and 무엇을 토대로 (based on what structure/foundation).
