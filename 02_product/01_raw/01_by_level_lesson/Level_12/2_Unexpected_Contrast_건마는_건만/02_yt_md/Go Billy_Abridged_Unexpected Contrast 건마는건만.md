## Teaching Points

### Overview

This lesson introduces the advanced grammar form 건마는 (or its shortened version 건만), which is used to connect two clauses with a sense of contrast. While it shares a basic functional meaning with the common connector 지만 (but/although), it carries a specific nuance of "unexpectedness." The instructor explains that this form is primarily literary and is used to highlight that the result in the second clause was not what one would logically expect based on the action or state described in the first clause.

---

### Conjugation and Tense Rules

To create this form, you simply take a verb stem and attach 건마는 or 건만. Both versions are identical in meaning and usage. The instructor demonstrates the conjugation using the verb 하다 (to do) across different tenses to show the specific structural requirements.

In the present tense, the ending attaches directly to the verb stem, resulting in 하건만. For the past tense, you use the past tense stem (the form used before adding 어요), resulting in 했건만. The future tense has a specific restriction: instead of using the common ㄹ 거예요 ending, you must use the 겠다 suffix. Therefore, the future tense conjugation becomes 하겠건만.

To help students internalize the grammar, the instructor suggests thinking of it as a direct substitute for 지만 in your head. For example:
- **하지만** (Present) → **하건만**
- **했지만** (Past) → **했건만**
- **하겠지만** (Future) → **하겠건만**

---

### Usage Nuances and Literary Feel

The primary function of 건마는 is to show contrast, similar to English words like "but," "however," "yet," or "although." However, there are two major distinctions between this form and the standard 지만. First, 건마는 is a literary form. It is rarely used in casual spoken conversation and is instead found in books, poetry, formal writing, or scripted media. It carries a "fancy" or "old-fashioned" bookish feel.

Second, the form carries a specific pragmatic nuance: it emphasizes that the second clause (B) was unexpected based on the first clause (A). The instructor frames the internal logic as: "A happened, however, what I did not expect was B." Because the outcome is unexpected, this form is very frequently used to express feelings of disappointment or regret, though these emotions are a result of the context rather than a literal meaning of the grammar suffix itself.

---

### Examples of Unexpected Outcomes

The instructor provides several example sentences to illustrate how the "unexpected" nuance works in practice. In each case, the second half of the sentence subverts the logical expectation established in the first half.

In a domestic context, one might say:
> **여긴 매일 청소하건만 낡아서 깨끗해 보이지 않아요.** / I clean this place every day, but (unexpectedly) because it's old, it doesn't look clean.
The expectation is that daily cleaning results in a clean look; the age of the building thwarts that expectation.

Regarding skill development:
> **피아노 연습을 계속하건만 아직 한 곡도 제대로 칠 수가 없어요.** / I keep practicing the piano, but (unexpectedly) I still can't even play one song properly.
Continuous practice should lead to proficiency, making the inability to play a single song a point of unexpected disappointment.

In a hypothetical scenario involving a gift:
> **여자친구가 원한다면 명품 가방도 사주겠건만 갖고 싶은 게 하나도 없대요.** / If my girlfriend wanted one, I'd even buy her a designer bag, but (unexpectedly) she says there isn't a single thing she wants.
The speaker is willing to provide an expensive gift, but the girlfriend's lack of desire for anything is the surprising turn.

Regarding business predictions:
> **오늘도 어제처럼 손님이 적을 거라고 생각했건만 제 예상이 틀렸어요.** / I thought there would be few customers today just like yesterday, but (unexpectedly) my prediction was wrong.

In a general observation about children:
> **사탕을 좋아하지 않는 아이는 없겠건만은 이 아이는 사탕을 정말 많이 좋아하네요.** / There wouldn't be any child who doesn't like candy, right? However (unexpectedly/emphatically), this child really likes candy a lot.
Note: In this specific example, the instructor uses the form to highlight the intensity of the child's preference against a general assumption.

Finally, regarding health and diet:
> **저도 저 가수랑 똑같이 먹건만 왜 체중이 줄어들지 않죠?** / I also eat the same as that singer, but (unexpectedly) why isn't my weight going down?
The expectation is that eating like a fit celebrity would result in weight loss; the lack of results is the unexpected contrast.
