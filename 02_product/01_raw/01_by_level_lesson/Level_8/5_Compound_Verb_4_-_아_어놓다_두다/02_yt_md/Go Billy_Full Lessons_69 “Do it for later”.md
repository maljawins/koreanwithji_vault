## Teaching Points

### Overview
The instructor introduces two auxiliary verbs, 놓다 and 두다, which are used to express the concept of performing an action in advance or for later use. While both verbs independently mean "to put something down," their function shifts significantly when attached to another verb. This lesson focuses on the grammatical construction and the specific nuance of preparation that these verbs add to a sentence.

---

### Grammatical Construction
To form this compound verb structure, the speaker must first conjugate the main verb into the standard 아/어/여 form (the same base used for the polite 요 form). Instead of adding 요, the speaker attaches either 놓다 or 두다. For example, the verb 청소하다 (to clean) becomes 청소해 when the 다 is dropped and the vowel is harmonized. By adding 놓다, it becomes 청소해 놓다. The instructor notes that while both are grammatically correct and interchangeable in most contexts, 놓다 is generally used more frequently in daily conversation.

---

### Meaning and Nuance: Preparation for the Future
The primary function of adding 놓다 or 두다 is to indicate that an action is being done "in advance" or "for later." It implies a state of readiness or preparation for a future event. The instructor illustrates this by comparing two sentences. A standard sentence like 오늘 집을 청소할 거예요 (Today I will clean the house) is a simple statement of intent. However, changing it to 오늘 집을 청소해 놓을 거예요 (Today I will clean the house for later) adds the specific nuance that the cleaning is being done as a preparation—perhaps because guests are arriving later. The focus is not just on the act of cleaning, but on the fact that the cleaning is being completed now so that the house remains in that state for a future purpose.

---

### Examples and Usage
The instructor provides several examples to demonstrate how this form applies to different verbs and situations. In the sentence 샌드위치를 만들어 놓을게요 (I'll make some sandwiches for later), the speaker is preparing food ahead of time. When using the imperative form, as in 내일까지 여행 갈 준비를 해 놓으세요 (Get ready to go on the trip by tomorrow), it emphasizes completing the preparation in advance of the deadline. The form can also be used with abstract verbs, such as 기억해 둘게요 (I'll remember that for later), where 두다 suggests keeping a piece of information "put away" in one's mind for future reference. Other examples include 신발을 여기에 벗어 놓을게요 (I'll take off my shoes here), implying they will be left there for a duration, and 오늘 밤까지 설거지를 해줘 (Do the dishes by tonight), which uses the base form to request completion of a task.
