## Teaching Points

### Overview

This lesson covers two advanced Korean verb forms used to express causality and attribution: 의하다 (to be due to/according to) and 인하다 (to be caused by). These forms are primarily found in formal, written contexts such as news reports, speeches, or legal documents. The instructor explains the etymological roots of these words—linking 의하다 to the concept of "leaning" or "relying" and 인하다 to "reasons" or "catalysts"—to help students understand their nuanced applications. The lesson also explores how these verbs conjugate into different grammatical roles, such as adverbs (의하면, 의해서, 인해서) and adjectives (의한, 인한), and provides a common alternative for citing sources.

---

### The Verb 의하다: According to and By

The verb 의하다 is a combination of the word 의 and the verb 하다. Its core meaning involves leaning on, relying on, or following something. When used in the form Noun에 의하면, it translates to "according to [noun]." This is a formal, written expression frequently used when quoting a source or a set of rules. The instructor notes a specific grammatical constraint: sentences using 의하면 must end in the present tense (or a present-tense form like the future ~ㄹ 거예요), even if the information within the quote refers to the past.

The instructor provides several formal examples to demonstrate this quoting function:
- **일기 예보에 의하면 내일부터 큰 비가 내릴 거예요.** / According to the weather forecast, a lot of rain will fall starting tomorrow.
- **헌법 제1조 1항에 의하면, 대한민국은 민주공화국이에요.** / According to Article 1, Section 1 of the Constitution, the Republic of Korea is a democratic republic.
- **관계자에 의하면, 이번이 처음이 아니라고 해.** / According to the person concerned, they say this is not the first time.
- **목격자 증언에 의하면, 잘못은 상대방에게 있어요.** / According to the witness's testimony, the fault lies with the other party.

---

### 의하다 in Passive Voice: By and Due To

When 의하다 is conjugated as Noun에 의해 or Noun에 의해서, it often functions within passive voice constructions to indicate the agent or the cause of an action. In these contexts, it translates to "by" or "due to." The instructor illustrates this with examples of things being controlled, rescued, or penetrated by an external force:
- **로봇에 의해 통제되는 세상** / A world controlled by robots.
- **바다에 빠진 강아지가 해경에 의해 구조되었어요.** / The dog that fell into the sea was rescued by the coast guard.
- **동굴에서 실종된 학생 세 명이 경찰에 의해 발견됐어요.** / Three students who went missing in a cave were discovered by the police.
- **해커 한 명에 의해 은행 전산망이 뚫렸어요.** / The bank's computer network was penetrated by a single hacker.

---

### The Verb 인하다: Caused By and Resulting From

The verb 인하다 is rooted in the concept of 인, which refers to a reason, cause, or catalyst. While 의하다 can mean "due to" in a general sense, 인하다 specifically highlights the actual reason that triggered an event. It is most commonly used as Noun+(으)로 인해(서) to mean "due to" or "as a result of."

An example of an apology for a caused situation is shown:
> **저로 인해 이런 일이 생겼다니 정말 죄송합니다.** / I am truly sorry that such a thing happened because of me.

When used as an adjective, the form becomes Noun+(으)로 인한. This describes a noun that is the result of another noun. For instance, **스트레스로 인한 불면증** translates to "insomnia caused by stress." The instructor mentions that while 인하다 and 의하다 can sometimes be swapped in active sentences (e.g., 스트레스에 의한 불면증), they are not interchangeable in passive constructions.

---

### Formal Alternative: Noun에 따르면

As a final tip, the instructor introduces Noun에 따르면 as a direct synonym for Noun에 의하면. This form comes from the verb 따르다 (to follow). Literally meaning "if you follow [noun]," it carries the exact same "according to" meaning and is also used in formal, written Korean. It can be swapped word-for-word with 의하면 when citing sources or information.
