**3. Consonant Assimilation with ㅎ**

* **ㅎ changes the pronunciation in 3 ways
  1) When ㅎ goes silent
  2) When ㅎ aspirates ㄱ ㄷ ㅂ ㅈ → ㅋ ㅌ ㅍ ㅊ
  3) When ㅎ 받침 turns into ㄷ (D) 받침**

**1) When ㅎ goes silent**

**ㅎ받침 + ㅇ**

* **ㅎ goes silent when followed by ㅇ (silent)**
* **Silent ㅇ neutralizes the aspirating sound of ㅎ**

**![](data:image/png;base64...)**

**Nasal (ㄴㄹㅁㅇ) 받침 + ㅎ**

* **Nasal 받침 sound neutralizes the aspirating sound of ㅎ**

**![](data:image/png;base64...)![](data:image/png;base64...)![](data:image/png;base64...)![](data:image/png;base64...)**

**2) When ㅎ aspirates ㄱ ㄷ ㅂ ㅈ**

* **When paired with ㄱ ㄷ ㅂ ㅈ, produces ㅋ ㅌ ㅍ ㅊ sound
  - ㅎ 받침 + ㄱㄷㅂㅈ = ㅋ ㅌ ㅍ ㅊ
  - ㄱㄷㅂㅈ 받침 + ㅎ = ㅋ ㅌ ㅍ ㅊ
   \*\* Aspirates ㄱㄷㅂㅈ either way**
* **ㅎ does NOT aspirate ㅅ because ㅅ does not have its own aspirated counterpart**

**ㅎ 받침 + ㄱ ㄷ ㅂ ㅈ**

* **ㅎ 받침 aspirates the following sound of ㄱ ㄷ ㅂ ㅈ
  \*\* There’s no word that starts with ㅂ after ㅎ 받침**

**4. SPECIAL OCCASION – PALATALIZATION**

**When ㄷ(D) 받침 & ㅌ(T) 받침 is met with 이 여 히 혀,**

* **ㄷ (D) turns into ㅈ (J)
  English Comparison: Did you → ‘D Jew**
* **ㅌ (T) turns into ㅊ (CH)
  English Comparison: Tuesday → Chews dei**
