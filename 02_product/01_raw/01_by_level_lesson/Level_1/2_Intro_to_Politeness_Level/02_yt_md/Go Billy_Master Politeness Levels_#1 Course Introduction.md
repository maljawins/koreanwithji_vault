### What Are Politeness Levels?
Politeness levels refer to the specific ways a speaker adjusts their language based on their relationship with the listener or the subject of the conversation. The instructor explains that this involves choosing to speak more or less formally depending on various social factors. For instance, a speaker would use a higher level of politeness when addressing someone they admire or respect, whereas they would use a more casual style with close friends.

The instructor identifies several key factors that determine which politeness level is appropriate: who you are speaking with (the listener), who you are speaking about (the subject), and the specific social situation you are in. Because these factors can be complex, the instructor warns that using politeness levels naturally is a long-term goal. Learners must understand the underlying social rules and the emotional weight of each style to avoid sounding awkward or unintentionally rude.
