Let’s look at some examples.

저는 클럽에 가지 않아요

않 ends in ㅏ, so it becomes 않아요.

저는 클럽에 가지 않아요

제 여자친구는 해산물을 좋아하지 않습니다.

In formal speech, 않다 becomes 않습니다 because 않 ends in a 받침.

By the way, the difference between 싫어하다 and negating 좋아하다 is very clear.

싫어하다 literally means “to dislike something” But 지 않다 simply negates 좋아하다 therefore it just indicates **the absence** of liking, which means 싫어하다 is slightly stronger than 좋아하지 않다.

저는 그 사람이 싫지가 않았어요.

As you can see, this is a TSV sentence

집에서 means “from the house” and 나가다 means to leave.

So, I didn’t leave the house yesterday.
