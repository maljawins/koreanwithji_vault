## Teaching Points
*   **Plant Analogy for Conjugation:**
    *   **Stem (하):** The unchanging base of the verb.
    *   **Branch (여):** The tense marker. For 하다, this is uniquely 여, which combines with the stem to eventually form 해.
    *   **Leaf (Context):** Represents the plain/informal state.
    *   **Fruit (요 / ㅂ니다):** The respect or formality marker.
*   **Formal High Conjugation:** In the formal high level (합니다), the branch connects directly to the "fruit" (ㅂ니다) without a "leaf" (context marker).
*   **Separable Noun + 하다 Verbs:** Action verbs that follow the Noun + 하다 format can often be separated into an Object + Verb structure (e.g., 공부하다 → 공부를 하다).
*   **Introduction to 에 and 있다:** A brief look at the particle 에 (at/on/in) and the verb 있다 (to exist/to be at a location), which will be the focus of the next session.
