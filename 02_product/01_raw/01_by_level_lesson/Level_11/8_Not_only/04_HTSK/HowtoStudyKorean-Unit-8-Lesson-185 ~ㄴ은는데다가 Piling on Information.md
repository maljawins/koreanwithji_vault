Lesson 185: ~ㄴ/은/는데다가: Piling on Information

Introduction

In this lesson, you will learn how to combine two grammatical principles that you learned in the past ~ㄴ/은/는데 and ~다가. Let’s get started.

~ㄴ/은/는데다가: Piling on Information

In [Lessons 76](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/upper-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-4-lessons-76-83/lesson-76/) and [77](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/upper-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-4-lessons-76-83/lesson-77/), you saw how you can add ~ㄴ/은데 to adjectives and ~는데 to verbs. You learned how you can use ~ㄴ/은데 or ~는데 to give the reader context or information for the upcoming clause. For example:

그 지역은 분쟁이 심한데 해결 방법을 찾기 어렵습니다
= The disputes in that region are severe, but finding a solution is difficult

In [Lesson 88](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/upper-intermediate-korean-grammar/unit-4-lessons-84-91/lesson-88/), you saw how you can use ~다가 in a very specific situations.

It is possible to attach ~다가 to ~ㄴ/은/는데. ~ㄴ/은데다가 can be attached to adjectives and ~는데다가 can be attached to verbs. When used, it implies that the speaker is piling one piece of information onto another. In doing so, the clause after ~ㄴ/은/는데다가 intensifies, or adds weight to the first one. For example:

Play[그 남자는 친절한데다가 성실하기까지 해요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s01.mp3)
= That man is not only kind but also sincere

You might be tempted to think that this is very similar to ~고 (Lesson 17). For example:

그 남자는 친절하고 성실해요
= That man is kind and sincere

In this example, the speaker’s nuance is neutral. It is simply two facts – that he is kind and sincere. When ~ㄴ/은/는데다가 is used, it adds an accumulative nuance. The second fact often makes the first situation feel worse or more impactful. Below are many more example sentences:

Play[오늘은 바람이 거센데다가 비도 많이 와요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s02.mp3)
= Today the wind is not only fierce but also it’s raining a lot

Play[이번 시험은 지원자가 많은데다가 경쟁도 치열해요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s03.mp3)
= This exam not only has many applicants but also has fierce competition

Play[그 선수는 실력이 뛰어난데다가 술수까지 잘 부려요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s04.mp3)
= That player is not only skilled but also good at tricks

Play[그 문제는 국제적 문제인데다가 해결도 쉽지 않아요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s05.mp3)
= That problem is not only international but also difficult to solve

Play[우리 아들은 못생긴데다가 비운의 사랑까지 겪었어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s06.mp3)
= Our son, not only not attractive but, on top of that, experienced a tragic love

Play[불법으로 동물을 포획하는데다가 해외로 팔아넘겼어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s07.mp3)
= They not only captured animals illegally but also sold them overseas

Play[그는 계획을 치밀하게 세운데다가 실행력도 뛰어났어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s08.mp3)
= He not only made his plans meticulously, but also had excellent execution skills

Play[그는 1차에서 탈락했는데다가 재도전 기회도 얻지 못했어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s09.mp3)
= He was not only eliminated in the first round but also had no chance to try again

Play[그 지역은 오랫동안 분쟁이 심한데다가 경제 상황도 나빠요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s10.mp3)

Play[그는 언론의 비난을 받고 있는 데다가 동료들의 신뢰도 잃었어요](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Lesson185s11.mp3)
