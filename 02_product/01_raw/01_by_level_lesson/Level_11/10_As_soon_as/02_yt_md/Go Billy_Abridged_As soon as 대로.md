## Teaching Points

### Overview

This lesson focuses on the versatile grammar point 대로, which carries several distinct meanings depending on how it is attached to verbs or nouns. The instructor breaks the lesson into three primary categories: using it with action verbs to mean "as soon as," using it with adjectives or verbs to mean "in the way/manner that," and attaching it directly to nouns to mean "in accordance with." Throughout the video, the instructor emphasizes the nuance of each form, comparing it to more common alternatives like 자마자 and highlighting specific usage constraints, such as the avoidance of negative verbs and future tense conjugations in certain contexts.

---

### Action Verb Stem + 는 대로: "As Soon As"

The first usage covered is attaching 대로 to an action verb stem using the 는 adjective-modifying marker. This construction functions similarly to the grammar point 자마자, meaning "as soon as." For example, the verb 하다 (to do) becomes 하는 대로, 먹다 (to eat) becomes 먹는 대로, and 살다 (to live) becomes 사는 대로. While the meaning is identical to 자마자, the instructor notes that 자마자 is far more common in everyday speech. The 는 대로 form sounds more formal and is typically found in speeches, formal writing, or grammar examinations rather than casual conversations with friends.

There are two critical rules for this specific usage. First, it cannot be used with negative verbs; one would not say 안 하는 대로 to mean "as soon as you don't do it." Second, it is most frequently used when conveying information—specifically when telling someone that as soon as one event occurs, another action (like calling or informing) will follow. This is demonstrated in the following examples:

> **숙제를 끝내는 대로 알려주세요.** / Please let me know as soon as you finish the homework.
> **해가 뜨는 대로 나갈 거예요.** / I will leave as soon as the sun rises.
> **철수가 도착하는 대로 전화 주세요.** / Please give me a call as soon as 철수 arrives.

While the past tense is possible (e.g., 먹은 대로), it is less common than the present tense form even when describing past events.

---

### Adjective/Verb + 대로: "In the Way/Manner That"

The second usage involves conjugating a verb (action or descriptive) into an adjective form to modify 대로, resulting in the meaning "as," "in the way," or "in the same manner as." This form is used to describe actions performed based on a specific experience, observation, or instruction. Unlike the "as soon as" meaning, this usage freely utilizes both past and present tense modifiers (e.g., 한 대로, 하는 대로, 했던 대로), but strictly avoids the future tense.

The instructor provides several examples to illustrate this "manner" meaning:
> **제가 요리한 대로 따라해 보세요.** / Try to copy it as I cooked it (in the way I cooked it).
> **이미 했던 대로 다시 하세요.** / Do it again just like you had already done before.
> **제가 하라고 한 대로 해봤어요?** / Did you try it as I told you to do it?
> **내가 생각했던 대로 잘 되지 않았어.** / It didn't go as well as I was thinking.

A special note is made for the descriptive verb 싶다 (to want/desire). Because it is a descriptive verb, it conjugates to 싶은 대로 rather than 씹는 대로.
> **하고 싶은 대로 하세요.** / Do it the way you want to do it.

Other common expressions using this pattern include:
- **약속한 대로** (as promised)
- **아는 대로** (as you know / everything you know)
- **들은 대로** (as you heard it)
- **기대했던 대로** (as expected / as one was expecting)
- **시키는 대로** (as told / as forced to do)

---

### Noun + 대로: "In Accordance With"

The final usage involves attaching 대로 directly to a noun. This translates to "just as/like the noun is/does" or "in accordance with." The instructor cautions that this cannot be done with just any noun; it is limited to specific nouns where the combination makes logical sense.

One of the most common examples is **마음대로**, which literally means "in accordance with your heart/mind," but is used to mean "whatever you want" or "as you please." In casual settings, this is often shortened to just **마음**.
> **마음대로 해요.** / Do whatever you want.

Other frequent noun combinations include:
- **순서대로** (in order / in accordance with the order)
- **말대로** (as [someone] says/said)
- **그대로 / 이대로** (as it is / like this)
- **예상대로** (as expected / as predicted)
- **계획대로** (according to plan)

Example sentences shown on screen:
> **순서대로 줄을 서서 기다려 주세요.** / Please stand in line and wait in order.
> **찰수의 말대로 영희도 파티에 올 것 같아요.** / Just as 찰수 said, I think 영희 will also come to the party.
> **그대로 냅두세요.** / Leave it as it is.
> **예상대로 늦게 끝났어요.** / It finished late as expected.
> **계획대로 흘러가고 있어요.** / It is flowing (going) according to plan.
