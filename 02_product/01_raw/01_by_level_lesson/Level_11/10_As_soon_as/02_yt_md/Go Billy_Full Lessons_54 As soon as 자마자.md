## Teaching Points

### Overview

The instructor introduces a simple and practical grammar form used to express the concept of "as soon as" in Korean. This structure allows speakers to link two actions where the second occurs immediately after the first, such as "As soon as he arrived, I wanted to leave." The lesson covers the basic conjugation rules, provides several example sentences to demonstrate usage in different contexts, and concludes with a short comedic skit to reinforce the meaning.

---

### Conjugation: Verb Stem + 자마자

The construction of this grammar form is straightforward and does not require complex changes based on batchim (final consonants). To create this form, the speaker takes the verb stem—which is the verb with the final 다 removed—and simply attaches 자마자. The instructor demonstrates this with two common verbs: first, 하다 (to do) becomes 하자마자 (as soon as I do); second, 먹다 (to eat) becomes 먹자마자 (as soon as I eat). Once this connector is attached to the first verb, the speaker completes the rest of the sentence to describe the subsequent action.

---

### Example Sentences

The instructor provides three distinct examples to show how the grammar functions in complete thoughts. The first example describes an immediate action upon arrival:

> **한국에 도착하자마자 김치를 사 먹었다.** / As soon as I arrived in Korea, I bought and ate kimchi.

The second example illustrates a change in weather conditions coinciding with an action:

> **밖에 나가자마자 비가 내리기 시작했다.** / As soon as I went outside, it started to rain.

The final example describes a quick transition into sleep:

> **침대에 눕자마자 잠이 들었다.** / As soon as I lied down in bed, I fell asleep.

---

### Practical Application Skit

The video concludes with a humorous role-reversal skit featuring Billy and his stuffed bear, Keykat. Initially, Keykat is sick in bed, and Billy promises to take care of everything. A title card then reads "One week later," showing Billy now sick in bed with a thermometer. Billy tells Keykat he is sick and asks for care. However, as soon as Keykat leaves the room, he immediately goes to the computer and begins typing and using the mouse vigorously. This visual sequence demonstrates the "as soon as" concept: as soon as the door closed and he was alone, Keykat stopped acting as a caregiver and started playing on the computer.
